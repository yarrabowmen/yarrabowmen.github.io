## Visiting Archery Australia affiliated club members

If you already have a membership with an Archery Australia affiliated club, you can shoot at Yarra Bowmen with **evidence of current financial membership** (down loaded from AA's Assemble portal). A standard **daily shooting fee of $10** is paid via the money box int he club house and you will sign in as per your home club. Our weekly club shoot is held every Sunday (0800 - 1100). Please check in with us with via [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/YarraBowmenBulleen) or email us at yarrabw@gmail.com to ensure access.


## Visiting Temporary Players/Participants

If you are not yet a member of an archery club, you need to provide **evidence of basic competence and shooting safety**, e.g. a certificate of an introductory archery course. A standard **daily shooting fee of $10** is paid via the money box next to the fridge and you will sign in as a temporary player.  Please check with committee members if anyone will be at the premises when you plan to arrive via [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/YarraBowmenBulleen) or email us at yarrabw@gmail.com. Sunday mornings are relatively reliable times (0800 - 1100) to meet members and shoot.

The Club does not offer any equipment hire.

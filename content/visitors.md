## Visiting Archery Australia affiliated club memebers

If you already have a membership with an Archery Australia affiliated club, you can shoot at Yarra Bowmen with **evidence of current financial membership** (down loasded from AA's Assemble portal). A standard **daily shooting fee of $10** is paid via the money box next to the fridge and you will sign in as per your home club. Please check with committee members if anyone will be at the premises when you plan to arrive via [our Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/YarraBowmenBulleen) or email us at yarrabw@gmail.com. Sunday mornings are relatively reliable times (0800 - 1100) to meet members and shoot.


## Visiting Temporary Players/Partcipants

If you are not yet a member of an archery club, you need to provide **evidence of basic competence and shooting safety**, e.g. a certificate of an introductory archery course. A standard **daily shooting fee of $10** is paid via the money box next to the fridge and you will sign in as a temporary player.  Please check with committee members if anyone will be at the premises when you plan to arrive via [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/YarraBowmenBulleen) or email us at yarrabw@gmail.com. Sunday mornings are relatively reliable times (0800 - 1100) to meet members and shoot.

The club does not accomadate any equipment hire.

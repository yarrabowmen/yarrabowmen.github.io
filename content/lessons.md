## Come and Try Sessions 

If you are new to archery and would like to try archery, contact Astin at [Mobile Archery](https://mobilearchery.com.au/) for a come'n'try session at the range. 

Another alternative is [Ambrose Archery](https://ambrosearchery.com.au/) to get stated indoors.  

Thereafter we hold a weekly shoot from 0800-1100 on Sunday mornings at the club. This is the best time to catch a committee member in person to talk about club membership should you choose to pursue archery beyond come'n'try sessions.
